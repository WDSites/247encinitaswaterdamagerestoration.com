Where Cardiff sits and why it matters

San Elijo Lagoon lies at the south edge of Cardiff. The lagoon is a 721-acre ecological reserve, according to the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, reached from Interstate 5 via Manchester Avenue. Near the lagoon edge or the beach, know where your water shut-off is.

Across the lagoon to the south is Solana Beach. North of Cardiff along the coast is Old Encinitas, and the city overview is on the Encinitas home page.

What the city's own records say about Cardiff water

The City of Encinitas hazard mitigation plan, hosted by San Diego County OES, says coastal flooding in Cardiff has caused property losses. It names the Restaurant Row area, south of the San Elijo State Beach Campgrounds, as flood-prone, and it notes flooding along Manchester Avenue near Mira Costa College. If you live or work near those spots, keep photos of your rooms and belongings on file before any storm.

A Coast News report on the city's stormwater study mentions a 2023 sinkhole on Lake Drive in Cardiff-by-the-Sea. The same report says parts of the underground system are more than 75 years old, so check where your downspouts and yard drains empty.

The city's identified hazards page lists Moonlight Beach and Cardiff-by-the-Sea as highly vulnerable to coastal inundation from future sea level rise. That describes a future risk, not an event that has already happened. Ask about flood insurance well before a storm, since a new federal policy typically takes 30 days to take effect.

Water damage scenarios in Cardiff

Storm and street water

Rain pushed under a garage door or a ground-level door can wet floors, boxes and baseboards. See storm water damage for how that differs from a flood.

Rising or standing water

If water came up from the ground or street in volume, read about flood damage restoration.

Sewage backup

A backup in a tub or floor drain is contaminated water. Read sewage backup cleanup before touching anything.

Plumbing inside the walls

A supply line, an appliance hose or a water heater can leak for hours before anyone notices.

Safety steps for the first hour

Stay safe first

Stay out of water that is near outlets, a panel or a gas appliance. Switch power off at the panel only when the panel itself is dry and you can reach it from dry ground. A breaker is never something to flip from a wet floor.

If you smell gas, see sparks or hear buzzing, leave and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

  1. Stop clean water at the fixture valve or the main shut-off if you can do it safely.
  2. Keep people and pets out of any water that came from a drain or from outside.
  3. Photograph the water line, the source and the wet belongings before you move anything.
  4. Lift small items and electronics off the floor, then call (760) 769-1159.

Sewer lines and who is responsible

The City of Encinitas runs the Cardiff Sanitary Division with city crews, according to its wastewater maintenance page. Repair and overflow cleanup on the private lateral, the line running from the building out to the street main, fall to the property owner. The city becomes accountable once sewage enters the main, so ask the city which side of the line the blockage is on.

What the crew does to extract and dry

The restoration crew that comes out checks the wet area with moisture meters, then removes standing water with pumps or wet-dry extraction units.

Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry the floors and wall cavities, and readings are taken until the materials reach a dry standard. Our structural drying page explains that stage. Contaminated water from a sewer or flood needs more than fans and an ordinary vacuum, and it is not a do-it-yourself job.

Documenting the loss for insurance

Photos, a written list of damaged items and moisture readings all help document a loss. Check your policy and ask your insurer how the water is classified, because the California Department of Insurance says homeowners and commercial policies typically exclude flood. Flood coverage is the federal National Flood Insurance Program, and we cannot say what any claim will pay.
