What we do

247 Encinitas Water Damage Restoration answers calls about burst pipes, ceiling leaks, flooding, sewage backups and mold, and sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to Encinitas, Cardiff-by-the-Sea, Leucadia, Olivenhain and nearby towns. Our office address is 499 N El Camino Real, Encinitas, CA 92024.

We keep the business simple. You call, you describe what you see, and we tell you what to do first. Then a technician assesses the property and explains the scope and the way the price is built before any work begins.

How a call works

  1. You call the dispatch lineThe line at (760) 769-1159 is answered 24 hours a day. We ask where the water came from, which rooms are affected and whether power or sewage is involved.
  2. We give first stepsYou hear how to stop the source, what to lift, what to photograph and when to stay out entirely.
  3. A technician assesses the propertyMoisture meters and thermal imaging show what is wet. You get the scope and the pricing method in plain terms.
  4. Work starts when you agreeExtraction, drying, cleanup or repair begins on your approval, and we record readings and photos along the way.

Standards we follow

The industry publishes two standards that matter here. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. They are published standards that any homeowner can ask any company about, and we encourage you to ask us how our crews apply them.

We work with licensed and insured professionals. California requires a contractor license for projects of $500 or more in combined labor and material, so we recommend checking any contractor's license on the Contractors State License Board website.

Equipment and methods in plain words

A water damage job relies on a short list of tools. Moisture meters measure how wet wood, drywall and concrete are, and thermal imaging cameras show cool, damp areas behind surfaces. Pumps and extraction units remove standing water, and air movers and dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the structure.

We use readings, not guesses. Drying is finished when materials return to their dry standard, and we show you the numbers. If something cannot be dried in place, such as saturated carpet pad or soaked insulation, we explain why and remove it.

How we think about price

Restoration pricing depends on facts that cannot be known from the phone: the water category, how much of the building is affected, which materials are involved, how many days of drying are needed and how much rebuilding follows. That is why we explain the scope and the pricing method after an on-site assessment and before work starts.

If the scope changes because hidden damage turns up, we tell you before extra work begins. You should never be surprised by a line on an invoice that was not discussed.

A note on mold

Mold needs moisture, and in homes it usually follows a leak or a humid cavity. We treat the water problem first, then contain and remove growth. Questions about health effects belong with a physician, and we do not diagnose or make health claims.

Ask whether your state licenses mold work before you hire anyone for it, and read our mold remediation page to see how a job is structured.

What we do not do

We do not stop water from entering a building. Waterproofing, grading and foundation work are different trades, and if your problem is recurring seepage we will say so and suggest the right specialist. We also do not make electrical or gas repairs, and we do not diagnose health conditions from mold.

We do not promise insurance outcomes. Coverage depends on your policy and your insurer's decision. What we can do is document the damage with moisture readings, photographs and an itemized scope, so your claim has accurate information behind it.
