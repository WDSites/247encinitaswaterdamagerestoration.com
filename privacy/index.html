Privacy Policy

What we collect, how calls are handled and what to expect from this website.

Overview

This policy explains how 247 Encinitas Water Damage Restoration, at 499 N El Camino Real, Encinitas, CA 92024, handles information. This website has no forms, no accounts and no checkout. The main way people contact us is by phone.

Call recording notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Information you may share on a call

When you call, you may give us your name, phone number, the address of the property and a description of the damage. We use that information to arrange service, answer your questions, keep records of what was discussed and improve how our dispatch line works.

You choose what to share. Some details, such as the address, are needed for us to confirm we serve your location and to send a technician.

Information collected by this website

Like most websites, the servers that deliver this site may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This helps keep the site secure and working.

This site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive technical information from your browser when a page loads, under their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email contact@247encinitaswaterdamagerestoration.com, we use your message and address to reply. Please do not send payment card details or other sensitive account numbers by email.

Your choices and changes

You can ask what information we hold about a call by phoning (760) 769-1159. We may update this policy from time to time, and the current version is always on this page.

For how we work and what to expect on a job, see the about page or the contact page.

Questions About Your Information?

Call dispatch at the number below and ask. We will explain what we have on file.
