What happens after the sewer line is cleared

Once Public Works has cleared or ruled out the main, the work inside the house begins. Technicians check what the water touched with moisture meters and thermal imaging, then pull standing water with extraction units. Porous materials that sewage reached, such as carpet pad and low drywall, are usually removed and not dried in place.

Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure. Readings are taken until the materials return to a dry standard. You are told the scope and the price up front, and the photos and notes support an insurance claim if you choose to file one.

Clean water jobs skip the disposal steps but follow the same drying logic. A burst hose in a Del Mar kitchen, for instance, means pulling water off the floor, checking cabinet bases and running drying equipment until the readings settle.
