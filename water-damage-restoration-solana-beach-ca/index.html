Before the crew arrives: keep the water from spreading

Block the entry

If rain is coming under a door, set towels or a rolled blanket at the threshold. Do not wade into a dark garage to do it.

Shut the plumbing source

A failed line needs its fixture valve or the main closed. Know where the main valve for your Solana Beach house sits before the next storm.

Cut power only when dry

Breakers for wet rooms can be switched off from a dry panel. If the panel area is wet, leave it alone and call the utility.

Raise belongings

Lift rugs, speakers, paper and anything on the floor onto dry furniture. Garage shelving near the floor is a common spot to forget.

Keep a photo log

Take photos of the entry point, the water line on walls and damaged items before cleanup, then call (760) 769-1159.
